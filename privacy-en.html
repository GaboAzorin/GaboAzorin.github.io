Mahjong Postcards Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Azor Games (Gabriel Azorín) (“we”), developer of the Android app “Mahjong Postcards” (Spanish name: “Mahjong Postales”). Contact: mahjong.postcards.support@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The game has no user accounts: we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or password.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To do so, Google may collect some device data (see section 4).
  • We do not use your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or files.
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores in its private app storage (not accessible to other apps): unlocked levels and stars, per-level game statistics (attempts, hints, shuffles, undos), postcards and medals earned, in-game coins, daily challenge results (date and time), local best times, and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, reduce motion, tutorial seen). This data is not sent to our servers (we have none) and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. If you have Android/Google device backup enabled, your progress may be included in that backup (managed by Google) and restored when you reinstall the app or switch phones; otherwise it is stored only on your device.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Optional rewarded ads are offered (for example, watching a video to get a hint) and interstitial ads (full-screen ads shown between boards, paced at intervals and never during a board).

When loading and showing ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and its advertising partners: IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), device and advertising identifiers (the Android advertising ID and App Set ID), interactions with ads and with the app, and diagnostics and performance data. Purposes: showing ads (personalized or not), measuring their effectiveness, analytics and fraud prevention. This data is transmitted encrypted. Google acts as an independent controller of that data; see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app declares the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and the Internet, network state and vibration permissions. The Google Mobile Ads libraries may add their own technical permissions (for example WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE) to show and measure ads; the app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Your choices:

  • Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where required): we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change it later with the “Privacy options” button in the app settings.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete it in Settings > Google > Ads, and limit ad personalization.
  • If you decline personalization you will still see ads, but not based on your profile.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. Data is only transmitted to the providers listed above (Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform, UMP) and only for the purposes described. Data may be processed on Google servers outside your country; Google provides safeguards for international transfers in line with applicable law.

6. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data; if you have Android/Google device backup enabled, any backup copy made by Google is kept according to Google’s own policies. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google’s own policies and retention periods.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the GDPR in the EU/EEA, or US state laws), you may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we have no accounts and do not store your personal data on our own servers, you can usually exercise these yourself (by clearing the app data and managing your advertising ID and consent). For any request, including data deletion, write to mahjong.postcards.support@gmail.com; we will reply within a reasonable time. Step-by-step instructions: how to delete your data. For data Google processes as its own controller, exercise your rights with Google.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country) and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Communications by Google SDKs are encrypted in transit. No system is completely secure, but we limit the data processed to the minimum necessary.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes (for example, when adding new features or services). We will publish the new version at this same address with its effective date.

11. Contact

Azor Games (Gabriel Azorín) · mahjong.postcards.support@gmail.com
